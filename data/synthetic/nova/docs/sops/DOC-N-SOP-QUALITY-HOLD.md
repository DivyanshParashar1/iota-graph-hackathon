# Quality Hold Procedure

Owner team: Quality. Version 1, last updated 2023-03-10.

A quality hold stops shipments of a part or product lot when inspection finds a defect rate above the agreed AQL. The Quality Engineer raises the hold and records the lot numbers.

Held stock is segregated. The supplier must deliver a corrective action report within five working days.

Release requires two consecutive lots inside the AQL and approval from the Supplier Quality Lead.
