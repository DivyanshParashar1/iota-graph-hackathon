# Dual-Sourcing Policy

Owner team: Procurement. Version 2, last updated 2024-11-15.

Version 2, updated 2024-11-15 after the September 2024 Shenzhen typhoon. Version 1 (2022) only required dual sourcing for parts above $1M annual spend.

What changed in version 2: every part with high criticality must have at least two qualified suppliers in different countries. A part that is single-sourced from China must have a qualified alternate outside China.

The Li-ion battery pack, previously single-sourced from our electronics supplier, now has qualified alternates and a three-week safety stock. Category Managers review single-sourced parts every quarter.

Exceptions to the dual-sourcing policy need approval from the Head of Procurement and are recorded as decisions.
