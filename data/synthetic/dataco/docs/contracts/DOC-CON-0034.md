# Supply Agreement: Smith PLC [SYNTH]

**Contract id:** CON-0034  
**Supplier:** Smith PLC [SYNTH] (SUP-T1-0086), DE  
**Relationship owner:** Hiro Tanaka [SYNTH] (EMP-003), Procurement  
**Term:** 2023-01-01 to 2027-12-31

1. Scope. The Supplier supplies the following parts to the Buyer: Button set type 1, Elastic band type 1, Thread spool type 2, Elastic band type 2, Thread spool type 3, Thread spool type 4. Purchase orders are issued monthly against a rolling forecast.

2. Lead time SLA. The Supplier delivers every purchase order within 45 days of the order date. The SLA is measured at the Supplier's export port.

3. Delay penalties. If a delivery is late beyond the 45-day SLA, the Supplier pays liquidated damages of 0.5% of the value of the affected orders for each day of delay, capped at 15% of that value. Penalties are invoiced after the incident is closed.

4. Force majeure. Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including typhoons, floods, earthquakes, port closures and government action, provided the Supplier notifies the Buyer in writing within five days. Carrier strikes are not force majeure events under this agreement.

5. Exclusivity. This agreement is non-exclusive. The Buyer may qualify and use alternate suppliers for any part.

6. Quality. Deliveries must meet the agreed AQL. Lots outside the AQL may be placed on quality hold at the Supplier's cost.

7. Termination. Either party may terminate with ninety days written notice, or immediately for a material breach that is not cured within thirty days.
