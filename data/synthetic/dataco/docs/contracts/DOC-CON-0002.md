# Supply Agreement: Davis and Sons [SYNTH]

**Contract id:** CON-0002  
**Supplier:** Davis and Sons [SYNTH] (SUP-T1-0005), MY  
**Relationship owner:** Victoria Kennedy [SYNTH] (EMP-005), Procurement  
**Term:** 2023-01-01 to 2027-12-31

1. Scope. The Supplier supplies the following parts to the Buyer: Printed fabric type 1, Foam core type 1, Team decal type 2, Printed fabric type 3, Aluminum tube type 3, Canvas sheet type 3. Purchase orders are issued monthly against a rolling forecast.

2. Lead time SLA. The Supplier delivers every purchase order within 21 days of the order date. The SLA is measured at the Supplier's export port.

3. Delay penalties. If a delivery is late beyond the 21-day SLA, the Supplier pays liquidated damages of 1.5% of the value of the affected orders for each day of delay, capped at 5% of that value. Penalties are invoiced after the incident is closed.

4. Force majeure. This agreement contains no force majeure relief for port closures, weather or carrier strikes. Delay penalties apply regardless of the cause of the delay.

5. Exclusivity. The Buyer sources these parts exclusively from the Supplier during the term.

6. Quality. Deliveries must meet the agreed AQL. Lots outside the AQL may be placed on quality hold at the Supplier's cost.

7. Termination. Either party may terminate with ninety days written notice, or immediately for a material breach that is not cured within thirty days.
