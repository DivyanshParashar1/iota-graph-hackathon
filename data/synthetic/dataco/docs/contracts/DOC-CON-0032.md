# Supply Agreement: Edwards Baker and Anderson [SYNTH]

**Contract id:** CON-0032  
**Supplier:** Edwards Baker and Anderson [SYNTH] (SUP-T1-0084), TR  
**Relationship owner:** Kimberly Brown [SYNTH] (EMP-004), Procurement  
**Term:** 2022-01-01 to 2028-12-31

1. Scope. The Supplier supplies the following parts to the Buyer: Charging cable type 3, Resistance cord type 3, Motion sensor type 5, Resistance cord type 5, Polymer casing type 6, PCB assembly type 7. Purchase orders are issued monthly against a rolling forecast.

2. Lead time SLA. The Supplier delivers every purchase order within 14 days of the order date. The SLA is measured at the Supplier's export port.

3. Delay penalties. If a delivery is late beyond the 14-day SLA, the Supplier pays liquidated damages of 1.0% of the value of the affected orders for each day of delay, capped at 15% of that value. Penalties are invoiced after the incident is closed.

4. Force majeure. This agreement contains no force majeure relief for port closures, weather or carrier strikes. Delay penalties apply regardless of the cause of the delay.

5. Exclusivity. This agreement is non-exclusive. The Buyer may qualify and use alternate suppliers for any part.

6. Quality. Deliveries must meet the agreed AQL. Lots outside the AQL may be placed on quality hold at the Supplier's cost.

7. Termination. Either party may terminate with ninety days written notice, or immediately for a material breach that is not cured within thirty days.
