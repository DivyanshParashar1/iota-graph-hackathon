# Customer Notification Policy

Owner team: Customer Ops. Version 1, last updated 2023-09-10.

Customers are told about a delay as soon as the new delivery date is known, and never later than the original delivery date.

Corporate segment customers get a named contact. Consumer segment customers get an email with the new date.
