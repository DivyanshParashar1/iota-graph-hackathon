# Contract Penalty Enforcement SOP

Owner team: Legal. Version 1, last updated 2023-02-10.

Delay penalties are calculated as the contract penalty percentage times the value of the affected orders times the days of delay beyond the SLA, capped at the contract cap.

Legal sends a penalty notice within thirty days of the incident close. Force majeure suspends penalties.
