# Carrier Strike Contingency SOP

Owner team: Logistics. Version 1, last updated 2023-06-10.

When a carrier announces a strike or a work stoppage, every lane operated by that carrier is at risk.

Move open orders to a lane with the same origin and destination operated by another carrier. Since 2023 no lane above fifty TEU per month may depend on a single carrier.

Notify Customer Ops of any order whose delivery date moves by more than two days.
