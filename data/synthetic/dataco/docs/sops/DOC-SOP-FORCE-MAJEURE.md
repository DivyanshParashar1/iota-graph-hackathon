# Force Majeure Claims Handling

Owner team: Legal. Version 1, last updated 2023-10-10.

When a supplier invokes force majeure, Legal checks that the event is listed in the contract clause and that the supplier notified us within the contractual window.

If force majeure applies, delay penalties are suspended for the duration of the event. If it does not apply, penalties accrue from the first day after the SLA.

Typhoons and port closures are force majeure events in most of our contracts; carrier strikes usually are not.
