# Incident Communication SOP

Owner team: Customer Ops. Version 1, last updated 2023-07-10.

During a supply incident the incident lead posts an update every four hours in the incident channel.

Every affected supplier's relationship owner is notified with the incident id, the numbers at risk and the relevant contract clause.

Customers with more than $10,000 at risk receive a personal call from Customer Ops.
