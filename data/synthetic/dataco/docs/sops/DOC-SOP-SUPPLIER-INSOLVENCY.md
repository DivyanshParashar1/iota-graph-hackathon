# Supplier Insolvency and Failure SOP

Owner team: Procurement. Version 1, last updated 2023-02-10.

This SOP covers a tier-1, tier-2 or tier-3 supplier that stops shipping because of insolvency, fire, flood or a regulatory shutdown. Procurement owns the response together with Quality.

Map every part that depends on the failed supplier directly or through the tier-2 and tier-3 network. A part is at risk when all of its active suppliers are affected.

Activate a qualified alternate supplier outside the affected region. Alternates must already be qualified; emergency qualification takes six weeks and needs the Supplier Quality Lead to sign off.

Place customer orders that cannot be covered on hold and inform Customer Ops.
