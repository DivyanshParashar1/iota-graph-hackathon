# Expedite Approval Matrix

Owner team: Logistics. Version 1, last updated 2023-04-10.

Expediting moves an order to a faster shipping mode, usually First Class or air freight. Every expedite has a cost delta that must be approved.

Up to $5,000 of extra freight: Transport Planner. Up to $50,000: Logistics Lead. Above $50,000: Head of Supply Chain and Finance.

Expedites caused by a supplier delay are charged back to the supplier when the contract has a delay penalty clause and force majeure does not apply.
