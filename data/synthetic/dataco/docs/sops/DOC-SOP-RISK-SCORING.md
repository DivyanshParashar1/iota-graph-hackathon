# Supplier Risk Scoring Method

Owner team: Procurement. Version 1, last updated 2023-01-10.

Supplier risk score combines reliability, country risk, single-sourcing exposure and network position.

Network position uses betweenness centrality over the supplier network: tier-2 suppliers that sit between many tier-3 and tier-1 suppliers are chokepoints and get a higher score.
