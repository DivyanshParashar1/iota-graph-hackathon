# Port Closure Response SOP

Owner team: Logistics. Version 1, last updated 2023-01-10.

This SOP applies when a port used by any of our lanes is closed or severely congested for more than 48 hours, for example by a typhoon, a strike or a customs shutdown. The Logistics Lead owns the response.

Step 1: confirm the closure with the carrier and the port authority. Step 2: list every open order whose lane starts or ends at the closed port. Step 3: compute revenue at risk and the customers affected.

Step 4: find an alternate port within trucking distance of the plant and an alternate lane to the destination. Reroutes that add three days or less of transit for fifty orders or fewer are pre-approved; anything larger needs approval from the Logistics Lead.

Step 5: notify the relationship owner of every affected supplier and the Customer Ops team. Step 6: run a postmortem within ten working days and update the playbook success rate.
