# Rerouting Approval SOP

Owner team: Logistics. Version 1, last updated 2023-12-10.

A reroute moves open orders from a disrupted lane to an alternate lane.

Reroutes adding three days or less of transit for fifty orders or fewer are executed automatically by the control tower. Larger reroutes wait in the action queue for approval by the Logistics Lead.

Every reroute records the before-state so it can be undone.
