# Alternate Supplier Qualification SOP

Owner team: Quality. Version 1, last updated 2023-08-10.

An alternate supplier is qualified for a part after a capability audit, a first article inspection and a pilot lot of at least 500 units.

Qualified alternates are recorded in the supplier master as QUALIFIED_FOR links and can be activated within two weeks.

Qualification expires after three years without an order.
